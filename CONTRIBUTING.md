**AION is a prediction mini-app** where humans go head-to-head with AI to forecast WLD price movements every 5 minutes. Built on WorldChain and powered by Allora’s open prediction tech, AION delivers real-time, data-driven insights in a transparent, gasless, and bot-free environment.

Follow the AI, go against it, or trust your own instinct — and earn instant rewards based on your accuracy. AION brings prediction to life through a fair, fast, and intelligent experience.

## Leveraging Allora Technology

To ensure fairness and transparency, we have integrated the core prediction technologies developed by Allora. The underlying tech, available publicly at [Allora GitHub Repository](https://github.com/allora-network/basic-coin-prediction-node), forms the backbone of our predictive analytics, enhancing accuracy and reliability in forecasting AION trends.

With Allora's robust framework, we aim to deliver unparalleled transparency in the prediction process, enabling informed decisions through open and fair use of advanced technologies. Explore the world of predictive analytics with confidence, powered by WorldChain and Allora.
